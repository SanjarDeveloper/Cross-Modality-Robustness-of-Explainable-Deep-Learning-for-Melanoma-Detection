## 4. Claims discipline applied

**Terminology**
- The protocol (§0.2) forbids "confirmatory". The synthesis uses **"primary retrospective analyses"** and notes where machine outputs use the older label.

**Uncertain or inconclusive results**
- Intervals that include zero are described as inconclusive, never as equality, equivalence or established inferiority. Examples: Stage 12A absolute IG vs centre, dermoscopic deletion, HL −0.007 [−0.017, 0.003]; E4.6; the RQ5 forgetting checks; DDI vs chance.

**What is and is not generalised**
- No generalisation to clinical deployment, provenance-verified smartphone images, causal modality effects or other models.

**Stated explicitly throughout**
- enriched cohorts;
- prior data exposure;
- retrospective additions;
- seed limitations;
- conditional and pointwise intervals.
