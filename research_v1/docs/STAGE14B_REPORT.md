## 5. Issues found in earlier materials (recorded, not silently changed)
1. **MASTER unit labels**
   - The heavy-minus-standard rows for the Derm7pt *AUROC* gap carry the unit "pp-scale proportion"; they are AUROC units.
   - The E4.5 top-10% overlap rows carry the unit "Spearman rho"; they are overlap fractions.

   The draft uses the correct units, and the ledger records the mislabels. `MASTER_RESULTS.csv` is unchanged.
2. **Ranking share for EfficientNet-B3 s1.** The value is 0.575 (`ranking_table.csv`); earlier text gave 0.58. The draft reports 0.575 to three decimals, because two-decimal float formatting gives 0.57.
3. **Grad-CAM under blur.** In the simulated arm, Grad-CAM's insertion advantage over random was *negative* under blur within its selected populations: −0.121 at 0.5 and −0.075 at 1.0. This is in the source data but was not stated in the 13A synthesis. It is now reported, as a description of selected subsets.
4. **Alternative-reference wording.** The 13A synthesis says advantages were "smaller and sometimes absent" against centre, Sobel and mismatch. This holds for deletion but not for insertion: Sobel, and for Grad-CAM the centre prior, were weaker insertion references than random. The draft states this precisely.
5. **"Single-institution"** (Methods v1). It is unsupported and potentially misleading, and has been corrected by a versioned note (§1).
